# ICA 2027: post-hoc RQ2/RQ3 analysis of completed experiments

## Scope and replication units

The analysis reads completed, frozen Qwen3-8B records only. Retirement comprises ten matched seeds at T30 and continuation of three of those same seeds to T60. GM-market support and generative-AI net-job-loss each comprise two matched seeds at T60. Every run has N=500 and six A0/A1 by B0/B1/B2 cells. The T30 panel contains 900,000 successful events; retirement continuation adds 270,000 and the two new topics add 720,000. The resulting unique network event total is 1,890,000. The T60 panel's reused retirement prefixes are not added again. Original P0 contributes 1,512 separate controlled one-step research cases, not network replications. Gemma and later P0c are excluded.

Matched seed is the independent replication unit. All primary displays retain seed values; A0/A1 averages within a seed are explicitly labeled. With two or three long-panel seeds, means/ranges and paired differences are descriptive. Event opportunities and message slots supply conditional denominators, not independent inferential sample sizes. No new p-values or seed-level confidence intervals are generated.

## Original-record reconstruction

Original archives and exact SQLite members are identified using frozen manifests. Extraction creates bounded temporary copies only in the new controlled V directory. Archive/member SHA-256 is checked, databases use immutable `mode=ro` plus `query_only`, connections close explicitly, and pre/post database hashes must agree. The original files, contracts and prior reports are never changed. No model request, experiment rerun or writable runtime factory is used.

Events are replayed in original global ordinal order. Before-state must match the preceding successful committed state. R0 initializes private states and public posts separately. A successful update changes private state; only an externally scheduled, actually committed publication changes the agent's public pointer. Actual feed slots are read from saved event input/selection evidence. Every slot is checked against the immutable historical publication, including R0 and older posts. Source current private state and latest public state are not substituted for a historical selected post. Tail ordinals start at15,000 and concatenate exactly one parent prefix. Stored available boundary snapshots and terminal states are compared with reconstruction; any unavailable separate snapshot record is documented, not invented.

## A1: flows, paths and formation

Round r contains original ordinals [(r-1)*500,r*500). These are 500 successful weighted-schedule activations, not one update per agent: the same agent can appear repeatedly and other agents need not activate in that observation block. Actual distinct actors and cumulative individual activation counts are retained. The 49 transition cells include diagonal self-retention. Off-diagonal inflow/outflow give net flow; cumulative net flow must equal current minus initial population for every stance. A population count sums to500 at every boundary. Direct i-to-j events, within-agent multistep paths, unique endpoint movers and repeated entries are distinct objects. First-entry and retained/reentered measures are labeled per agent and target stance; an agent starting at the target has baseline entry ordinal -1/round0. Seven-bin labels preserve the original topic-specific ordinal ordering.

## A2: realized-exposure response

Conditional response cells count events, not slots. Pure-j means a nonempty actual feed with every selected public post at stance j. Exact i-to-j updates and any movement toward j are separate numerators on the same eligible event denominator. No eligible events yields NA, not zero. More general realized exposure records pure, same/opposite-side majority (at least two-thirds), mixed, neutral-start and no-feed categories; these are post-hoc operational descriptions, not measured psychological mechanisms. Exact feed-minus-prior distance is retained as numerator/feed-length, avoiding arbitrary rounding. Cumulative exposure, repeated sources/publications, source-group composition and message ages use only preceding realized exposures. Endogenous feed and prior history prevent causal interpretation of these conditional associations.

## B1-B3: global, local and group-cue contrasts

Private variance uses the population definition (`ddof=0`). Network effect is variance(B)-variance(B0), paired by topic/seed/identity/horizon. Topology contrast is variance(B2)-variance(B1), equivalent to the difference of their paired B0-relative effects. Percentage differences explicitly divide by matched B0 variance. Change since R0, normalized changes and signed per-round speeds are retained separately.

Undirected edge similarity includes same-stance fraction, absolute ordinal distance and properly symmetrized endpoint Pearson assortativity; degenerate denominators are NA. Original graphs are fixed. For each round, an unrestricted node-label permutation retains all seven population counts; a within-Blue/Green version also retains group-specific counts. The expected same-stance probability is calculated exactly from these margins. Deterministic 199 permutations with analysis seed20261101 additionally describe null spread. These are post-hoc analytic settings, not new experimental conditions; null quantiles are not seed-level confidence intervals or formal p-values. Degree-stratified permutations are omitted to keep the requested offline analysis bounded. Actual-feed homophily is calculated separately from graph-edge homophily.

A1-A0 contrasts and identity-by-network difference-in-differences use matched seed/issue/horizon. B0 has no social edges: its edge statistics and corresponding edge DID are undefined, never zero-filled. The B2-versus-B1 contrast of identity effects is a defined network-pair alternative, not a fabricated B0 DID.

Original Stage-A/B joint manipulation admission did not pass and its verdict is unchanged. A0/A1 results describe the actual joint condition contrast and do not establish an identity, trust or group-identification mechanism. B1/B2 change realized content and repeated-source exposure as well as topology, so contrasts do not isolate topology at equal dose. Retirement used a different context/material version than the new topics; cross-topic comparisons are descriptive rather than pure issue interventions.

## B4: four information layers

Private opinion and latest public stock each use500 agents. Public stock retains exactly each agent's latest published post, including its R0 post; it is not the cumulative archive. New public flow counts publications within a round; realized exposure counts actually selected message slots. Empty flows/exposure have zero counts but undefined proportions/moments. Comparisons include mean absolute within-agent private/public gap, total variation, ordinal Wasserstein distance, public-stock turnover, age and repeated exposure. Age thresholds at1,5,10 rounds are explicitly post-hoc descriptive bins.

## A3 and privacy

150 numerically stratified genuine events form a human-review casebook. Selection balances topic, seed, identity/network, updating/retention and exposure patterns without automatically assigning semantic mechanisms. Exact before/after reasons and selected feed text remain in the controlled full evidence package. Public indices and the Review Packet exclude private full texts, databases and credentials. Selective agreement, social alignment and recursive influence require distinct evidence; merely approaching a dominant stance does not establish sycophancy. Semantic categories remain pending human assessment unless supported by an explicit audited reading.

## Reproducibility and gaps

Code, input signatures, per-run counts and table hashes accompany outputs. The Review Packet contains aggregated/seed-level evidence and minimum code; it does not pretend that small aggregate tables alone replace controlled raw archives. Any unresolved source field or mismatch is logged separately, and unaffected feasible analyses continue. Previously frozen outcomes are not silently overwritten if a discrepancy is found.
